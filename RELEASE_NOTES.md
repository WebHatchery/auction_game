# Auction House Tycoon 0.1.0 release candidate

Auction House Tycoon is a touch-friendly property strategy game for browser and Windows.

## Included

- A deterministic campaign from market research through live auctions, settlement, leasing, renovation, rent review, refinance, sale, and final ledger.
- Rival personalities, vendor counteroffers, auction-room reads, save/load migration, and browser-safe suspended-tab timing.
- Procedural auction and interface feedback with persisted master, music, and sound-effect volume controls.
- A persisted text-size setting for readability, plus visible controls sized for mouse and touch play.

## Campaign goal

Before week 24, own three homes, earn $1,500 in weekly rent, and hold $240,000 net worth.

## Platforms

- Browser: HTML5/WebAssembly build.
- Windows: downloadable desktop build.

## Known boundaries

The game is designed around a 1200×675 virtual canvas and scales its text and presentation to the available window. A physical keyboard is not required for the campaign.
