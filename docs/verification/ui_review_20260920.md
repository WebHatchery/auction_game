## Findings verified in the pass

- Preparation keeps the selected cap, researched range, finance, and registration
  decision together; complete property and sale explanations are behind explicit
  `READ REPORT` controls with visible close actions.
- Portfolio keeps the selected holding dominant, makes maintenance and rent review
  the first workspace, and hides finance, sale, contractor, and marketing choices
  behind their relevant disclosures. The dense eight-home capture shows reachable
  five-item paging.
- Auction finance warnings stay beside `RAISE` and `JUMP`; the finance capture
  shows both disabled-price reasons without restoring a permanent finance panel.
  Lobby price rows state their opening-bid, next-bid, cap, and outcome basis.
- The initial dashboard collapses the absent weekly statement. Closed-week and
  shortfall captures show the statement and debt recovery route without giving the
  first listing an arbitrary selected treatment.
- Risk labels use the shared researched-knowledge boundary. A Street Scan remains
  unverified; Agent Pack and later research can earn low, moderate, or elevated
  findings. `tests/research_knowledge.rs` covers the thresholds and inspection
  boundary.
- Sale and campaign explanations preserve the money path and expose full wrapped
  autopsies without adding a permanent competing panel. Help is reopenable from
  Menu and names the current screen's controls.

## Input and resize notes

All game buttons and rectangle-clicked controls use the toolkit's scale-aware
44-point hit-area helper. Settings now shares the same virtual-UI pointer mapping
as gameplay. The capture pass confirms visual placement at the normal, minimum,
and historical sizes; it does not constitute a physical touch-device or live
browser interaction test. Those remaining checks stay in `TODO.md` as UI-06,
UI-V1, and UI-V2.
